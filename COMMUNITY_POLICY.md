# Orbit Community Contribution Policy

[English](#english) | [中文](#中文)

## English

Orbit uses a review gate for collaborative training. A submitted or imported contribution is never added directly to training data.

1. The contributor declares the content's source or reuse rights and confirms that unnecessary personal information has been removed.
2. Orbit performs a conservative pre-screen. A flag places the contribution in quarantine; it is not a legal or factual verdict.
3. A local human reviewer checks relevance, rights, privacy, safety, and quality. Factual material also requires an HTTPS source and explicit fact/source verification.
4. Only an approved contribution can be assembled into a training dataset. Quarantined content cannot be directly approved; it must be corrected and resubmitted.
5. Review records and content stay on the user's computer unless the user explicitly exports and shares a contribution package.

No automated filter can guarantee that every statement is true or that every contribution is lawful in every jurisdiction. Project owners remain responsible for review and local compliance.

The workflow is informed by official data-governance principles concerning lawful sources, intellectual property and personal information, truth and accuracy, labeling quality, and human quality review:

- [Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services — Cyberspace Administration of China](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm)
- [Provisions on the Governance of the Online Information Content Ecosystem — Ministry of Industry and Information Technology / CAC](https://hnca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2020/art_090ebc6efc9e4faeb68466d18644b299.html)
- [EU Artificial Intelligence Act, data and data-governance requirements — EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng)

## 中文

Orbit 对协作训练采用审核闸门。任何新提交或导入的贡献都不会直接加入训练数据。

1. 贡献者声明内容来源或再利用权利，并确认已经移除不必要的个人信息。
2. Orbit 进行保守的机器预筛。命中规则只会把内容放入隔离区，不代表已经作出法律或事实判断。
3. 本机人工审核人检查相关性、权利、隐私、安全和质量。事实材料还必须附 HTTPS 来源，并明确确认已核实事实和来源。
4. 只有“已批准”的贡献才能汇总为训练数据。隔离内容不能直接批准，必须修正后重新提交。
5. 内容和审核记录默认保留在用户电脑上；只有用户主动导出并分享贡献包时才会离开本机。

任何自动过滤器都无法保证所有陈述一定真实，也无法保证内容在所有司法辖区都合法。共同模型的维护者仍需承担审核和当地合规责任。

该流程参考了中国网信部门关于合法数据来源、知识产权、个人信息、真实性准确性、标注质量与人工抽查等要求，以及欧盟《人工智能法》的数据治理原则；官方原文链接见英文部分。
